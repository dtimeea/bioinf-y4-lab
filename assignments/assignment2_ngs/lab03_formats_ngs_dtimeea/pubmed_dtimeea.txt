--- Articol 1 (PMID: 42992548) ---
Titlu: Metabolic reprogramming of myeloid‑derived suppressor cells in colorectal cancer: Mechanistic insights and immunometabolic targeted rehabilitation (Review).
Autori: Zhu LX, Jiang GJ, Gao FH, Du YQ
Rezumat:
Metabolic reprogramming of myeloid‑derived suppressor cells (MDSCs) plays a role in immunotherapy resistance in colorectal cancer (CRC). This metabolic reprogramming appears to be coordinated by oncogenic mutations (such as adenomatous polyposis coli, KRAS and TP53) through a proposed self‑reinforcing 'mutation‑metabolism‑suppression' axis. In the present review, four core metabolic pillars sustaining MDSC pathogenicity and the epimetabolic regulation that durably locks them into a suppressive state were examined. Furthermore, two emerging dimensions were highlighted: The spatial metabolic antagonism through which MDSCs may construct a metabolically hostile zone to limit the functional activity of tertiary lymphoid structures, and the remote manipulation of MDSC function by gut and intratumoral microbiota‑derived metabolites. To transcend the limitations of non‑specific myeloid depletion, immunometabolic rehabilitation is proposed as a potentially transformative framework targeting intrinsic metabolic vulnerabilities to functionally reprogram MDSCs from an immunosuppressive to a homeostatic or immunostimulatory phenotype. By integrating emerging spatial omics for precise target validation, artificial intelligence‑guided rational drug design and microbiome modulation, this paradigm provides a strategic roadmap for next‑generation precision immunotherapy, aiming to achieve durable responses across both microsatellite stable and microsatellite instability‑high CRC.

--- Articol 2 (PMID: 42607849) ---
Titlu: RPA3 regulates NSCLC malignant phenotypes through epistatic functional association between GPR87 and p53.
Autori: Yu Y, Gao W, Liu W, Ma H, Wang H
Rezumat:
This study aimed to clarify the functional regulatory links among RPA3, GPR87 and p53 in the tumorigenesis and progression of non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Bioinformatic analysis and Western blot assays were performed to evaluate the expression profiles of RPA3 and GPR87 in NSCLC. CCK-8, EdU, wound healing, Transwell and flow cytometry assays were used to explore the effects of RPA3 knockdown on NSCLC proliferation, migration, invasion, cell cycle and apoptosis. Both pharmacological inhibition and siRNA-mediated knockdown of p53, together with GPR87 overexpression, were applied for in vitro and in vivo functional rescue verification. The results showed that RPA3 and GPR87 were highly expressed in NSCLC tissues and cells. RPA3 silencing inhibited cell proliferation, induced apoptosis and caused S-phase arrest in A549 and H23 cells. Notably, both GPR87 overexpression and genetic/chemical inhibition of p53 effectively rescued the tumor-suppressive phenotypes induced by RPA3 depletion. Cellular and xenograft experiments consistently confirmed that RPA3 modulates NSCLC malignant behaviors through functional phenotypic linkage of GPR87 and p53. In summary, RPA3 exerts oncogenic effects by regulating GPR87 and p53-dependent cellular functions, thereby promoting NSCLC cell proliferation, suppressing apoptosis and facilitating tumor growth.

--- Articol 3 (PMID: 42960272) ---
Titlu: Li-Fraumeni syndrome presenting with early-onset eyelid sebaceous carcinoma.
Autori: Wang LL, Choi S, Kim H, Kwon HJ, Kim N
Rezumat:
PURPOSE: To report a rare case of Li-Fraumeni syndrome (LFS) presenting with early-onset eyelid sebaceous carcinoma in an Asian patient. OBSERVATIONS: A 36-year-old Korean woman presented with a gradually enlarging left lower eyelid mass. Biopsy confirmed sebaceous carcinoma. Her medical history included acute promyelocytic leukemia at age 29, glioblastoma at age 33, and parotid gland cancer at age 34. Wide excision with flap reconstruction was performed, and immunohistochemistry excluded Muir-Torre syndrome. Genetic testing revealed a heterozygous pathogenic germline TP53 variant, establishing the diagnosis of LFS. CONCLUSIONS: This is an Asian case of eyelid sebaceous carcinoma associated with genetically confirmed LFS. It expands the tumor spectrum of LFS and highlights the need to consider genetic counseling and testing in young patients presenting with sebaceous carcinoma, particularly when accompanied by multiple primary malignancies or a relevant family history. Early recognition of LFS is essential for personalized management and surveillance of both patients and at-risk relatives.

--- Articol 4 (PMID: 42951392) ---
Titlu: Human papillomavirus-independent, TP53 Wild-type Vulvar Intraepithelial Neoplasia and Its Mimics.
Autori: Kang Y, Skala SL
Rezumat:
High-risk human papillomavirus-independent, p53-wild-type vulvar intraepithelial neoplasia (HPVi(p53wt) VIN) is a squamous cell carcinoma precursor lesion with altered squamous maturation but minimal to no cytologic atypia. Use of p16 and p53 immunohistochemical stains is strongly encouraged for accurate subclassification of VIN lesions due to the potential for overlapping morphologic features. Additionally, there are a number of benign and inflammatory mimics of HPVi(p53wt) VIN. For lesions that cannot be definitively classified as either neoplastic or inflammatory/reactive/benign, descriptive diagnoses or "vulvar aberrant maturation" are appropriate.

--- Articol 5 (PMID: 42948049) ---
Titlu: DNA polymerase epsilon-mutant endometrial carcinosarcoma: A case report and literature review.
Autori: Zhang Y, Huang B, Ge H
Rezumat:
DNA polymerase epsilon (POLE)-mutant endometrial carcinosarcoma (ECS) is a rare clinical lesion. The current study describes the diagnosis and treatment of a woman who presented with yellow vaginal discharge for >2 months; in addition, a literature review was conducted, and the clinical and pathological characteristics, treatment and prognosis of POLE-mutant ECS are discussed. Preoperative endometrial curettage was performed and the patient was diagnosed with poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma. Based on this finding, a total hysterectomy, bilateral adnexectomy and lymph node dissection were performed. A postoperative pathological examination revealed the morphological characteristics of a high-grade carcinosarcoma in the endometrial tumor. The results of immunohistochemical staining showed that the carcinoma was cytokeratin 7-positive (sarcoma, -) and both the carcinoma and sarcoma were vimentin-positive (carcinoma, weak +; sarcoma, +). Additionally, TP53 showed aberrant immunohistochemical overexpression in the carcinoma and sarcoma, and the Ki-67 proliferation index was 80% in carcinosarcoma. Molecular testing revealed a c.1231G>T (p.Val411Leu) mutation in exon 13 of the POLE gene, and mutations in the TP53 gene were detected in exons 5, 6 and 8. Adjuvant therapy was not administered to the patient after surgery, and no recurrence/metastasis was detected during the 18-month follow-up period. These findings indicate that molecular subtyping should be performed routinely in ECS to identify the POLE-mutant subtype and to avoid overtreatment.
